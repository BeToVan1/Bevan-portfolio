MyCineList

A full-stack film discovery and collection app with Google sign-in, 1–10 ratings, watchlists, and shareable movie lists, now hosted on Vercel.

Node.js / Express / EJS / MongoDB / TMDB / Vercel

Discover your next film

The updated discovery screen combines a featured film and curated shelf with genre filtering, now-playing movies, and live search against the TMDB catalog. Movie data includes posters, descriptions, and release information.

Build and share a collection

Google OAuth connects sign-in to persistent user data in MongoDB. Users rate films on a 1–10 scale, keep a watchlist, sort personal lists by title or score, and share their movie collection through a public list link.

Static delivery, dynamic features

An EJS build renders the Discover page ahead of time for delivery through the Vercel CDN. Search, authentication, and saved-list requests run through Node.js and Express on Vercel Functions. MongoDB stores account data and sessions, while TMDB provides the movie catalog.
