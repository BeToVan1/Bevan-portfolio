Red Goes Fasta!

A 2D top-down racing game built in Unity and C# by a team of four.

My contribution

I implemented AI-controlled cars to race against players and scripted core mechanics including acceleration, drifting, and shooting.

Building the race

Player driving mechanics and AI-controlled opponents form the core racing loop. Acceleration and drifting govern car movement, while shooting adds another interaction during the race.

Testing through play

I ran playtesting sessions to find and document bugs. Those findings fed into iterative balance and performance fixes, connecting gameplay feedback with implementation work.
